Setup

Tempo, length and taper restart the run. Torque and drag apply immediately.

Model: planar three-joint arm, each joint a torque-limited motor with a linear torque/speed curve tracking minimum-jerk keyframes; the whip pulls back on the joints. The whip is 56 lumped masses with inextensible links, bending stiffness EI ∝ d⁴, quadratic air drag and a floor, integrated at 20 kHz with extra substeps near the crack. The taper does the work: the travelling loop moves into ever lighter material and speeds up.

Aimed strike: the card (88 mm, 1.8 g) hangs edge-on on a 30 cm string, 2.6 m in front of the shoulder axis. Strike end pose, strike duration and wind-up angle were optimised offline for card heights from 0.5 to 2 m (tools/aim.js) and are interpolated in between. A hit is the tip or the last two whip nodes sweeping through the card within 4 mm; the card does not push back on the whip.

Speed of sound taken as 343 m/s (20 °C, sea level).
